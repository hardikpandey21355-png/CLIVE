Frequently Asked Questions

How to Log In to Clive

Logging into Clive is quick and secure. Follow these simple steps:

Step 1: Open the Clive app or visit the website
Step 2: Click on the "Login" button at the top right
Step 3: Enter your registered email address
Step 4: Type your password
Step 5: Click "Sign In" and you're ready to chat!

Tip: If you forgot your password, click "Forgot Password" to reset it via email.

How to Log Out of Clive

To safely sign out of your Clive account:

Step 1: Click on your profile picture or name in the top right corner
Step 2: A dropdown menu will appear
Step 3: Select "Log Out" from the menu
Step 4: Confirm if prompted

Note: Logging out will end your current session. Make sure to save any important information before signing out.

How to Purchase a Paid Plan

Upgrade to Clive Premium for unlimited access and exclusive features:

Step 1: Click on "Upgrade" or "Get Premium" in the sidebar
Step 2: Choose your plan (Monthly or Yearly — Yearly saves 20%!)
Step 3: Click "Subscribe Now"
Step 4: Enter your payment details (Credit Card, PayPal, or UPI)
Step 5: Confirm payment and enjoy premium features instantly!

Benefits: Unlimited messages, priority support, advanced AI models, and no ads.

How to Archive Chats

Keep your chat list clean by archiving old conversations:

Method 1 — Single Chat:
• Right-click on the chat you want to archive
• Select "Archive Chat" from the menu
• The chat will move to your Archive folder
Method 2 — Multiple Chats:
• Long press (mobile) or Ctrl+Click (desktop) to select multiple chats
• Click the Archive icon at the top

To View Archived Chats: Go to Settings → Archived Chats. Your chats are safely stored and can be restored anytime.

How to Use "Merge Chats" Feature

Combine multiple conversations into one seamless chat:

Step 1: Open the "Merge Chats" option from the main menu or sidebar
Step 2: Select the chats you want to merge (minimum 2, maximum 5)
Step 3: Arrange them in your preferred order by dragging
Step 4: Choose a title for your merged chat
Step 5: Click "Merge" — All messages will be combined chronologically!

Perfect for: Combining project discussions, trip planning, or research from multiple conversations.

How to Retrieve Past Chats

Access your conversation history anytime:

Method 1 — Browse:
• All your chats are automatically saved in the sidebar
• Scroll through to find old conversations
• Use the search bar at the top of the chat list
Method 2 — Search Within Chats:
• Open any chat and press Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac)
• Type keywords to find specific messages

Cloud Sync: Your chats are synced across all devices. Log in anywhere to access your full history.

How to Set Password on Clive

Secure your Clive account with a strong password:

During Sign Up:
• Create a password with at least 8 characters
• Use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
• Avoid common words or personal information
Change Existing Password:
Step 1: Go to Settings → Security
Step 2: Click "Change Password"
Step 3: Enter your current password
Step 4: Type your new password twice
Step 5: Click "Update Password"

Security Tip: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for extra protection in Settings → Security.

How to Change Background Video

Customize your chat experience with animated backgrounds:

Step 1: Click on Settings (gear icon)
Step 2: Select "Appearance" or "Themes"
Step 3: Choose "Background Video" option
Step 4: Browse our library of live wallpapers (nature, abstract, gradients)
Step 5: Click "Apply" and adjust opacity & blur to your liking

Note: Background videos may use more battery. Switch to static wallpaper to save power.

How to Change Wallpaper

Personalize your chat background with static images:

Step 1: Go to Settings → Appearance
Step 2: Click on "Wallpaper"
Step 3: Choose from Solid Colors, Gradients, Images, or upload your own
Step 4: Adjust brightness and contrast so text remains readable
Step 5: Click "Save" to apply

Pro Tip: Darker wallpapers work best for night mode and reduce eye strain.

How to Customize Message Bubble

Make your chats uniquely yours with custom message bubbles:

Step 1: Open Settings → Chat Settings
Step 2: Select "Message Bubbles"
Step 3: Customize Colors, Shapes, Transparency, and Borders
Step 4: Customize received messages separately
Step 5: Preview changes in real-time
Step 6: Click "Save Changes"

Special Effects: Premium users can add animated bubbles, 3D effects, and custom fonts!
