About Clive_V1

Clive is an advanced AI assistant designed to think critically, act logically, and communicate with precision. It combines deep technical knowledge with practical problem-solving ability, making it ideal for students, developers, and professionals.

Clive challenges assumptions, provides counter-arguments, and focuses on truth rather than agreement. It can write code, debug, analyze complex topics, guide projects, and assist with research — all while adapting to the user's style and goals.

Clive is reliable, fast, and built for real-world productivity.

Delete Account

This action is permanent.

  • • All your chats will be deleted forever
  • • Your account will be removed from our servers
  • • Logging in again will create a fresh empty account

If deletion requires re-login, sign in again and click delete once more.

Delete All Chats

This action is permanent.

  • • Every chat you have ever created will be removed
  • • This cannot be undone or recovered
  • • Your account will remain active

Only your chats will be deleted. Your account and settings will stay intact.
