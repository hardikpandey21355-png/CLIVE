    Delete Account

    This action is permanent.

    • • All your chats will be deleted forever
    • • Your account will be removed from our servers
    • • Logging in again will create a fresh empty account

    If deletion requires re-login, sign in again and click delete once more.

    Delete All Chats

    This action is permanent.

    • • Every chat you have ever created will be removed
    • • This cannot be undone or recovered
    • • Your account will remain active

    Only your chats will be deleted. Your account and settings will stay intact.

    Delete All Chats?

    This will permanently delete every conversation from your account.

    This action cannot be undone and your chats cannot be recovered.
